1. Why is this smarter than normal CCTV cameras?

Ordinary surveillance cameras only record video silently. If an intruder climbs over your fence or an emergency occurs at night, a traditional CCTV system simply records the damage—leaving you to discover it only the next morning!

Aethra Vision transforms your ordinary cameras into an awake, living electronic security guard:

  • Instant Alarm Notification: The moment someone steps into a restricted area, our AI recognizes the human shape immediately and raises an alarm on the screen.
  • Human Voice Warnings: The computer actually speaks out loud in clear human speech (both English & Sri Lankan Sinhala) to warn guards and deter intruders!
  • Audio Threat Listeners: It actively listens for violent sounds like aggressive screaming or window glass breaking before anyone even steps in front of a camera lens.

2. What Equipment Do You Need? (Setup Checklist)

You do not need multimillion-dollar supercomputers to use this system. It runs on reliable, standard office equipment:

📹 Standard Network Cameras

Any modern IP CCTV camera connected via an Ethernet cable (RTSP / ONVIF enabled). No special hardware cameras required.

💻 Regular Office Computer

An Intel Core i5 or i7 computer with 16 GB RAM and an NVIDIA graphics card (like an RTX 3050 or 3060) to run real-time AI smoothly.

💾 Hard Disk Storage

One camera uses around 27 GB per day for continuous recording. A normal 4 TB or 8 TB Surveillance Hard Drive can safely archive over a month of videos for 4 to 8 cameras!

⚡ Smart Motion Storage

When our WORM Smart Feature is turned on, the system only records when active motion is detected—saving up to 65% of your hard drive space!

4. One-Click Superpowers You Should Try

  • 🌙 Night Vision Goggles & 🔥 Thermal Map: On any live video box, simply click the top switch labeled ⚡ RGB NORMAL. Watch your screen instantly change into military-grade Night Vision Green (NVG) or Heatmap Thermal Vision to spot people in total darkness!
  • 🚨 Emergency Facility Lockdown Button: Located at the top right of your screen in glowing red. Clicking this immediately activates red strobe flashers across your entire application and commands your computer speakers to broadcast emergency sirens and voice warnings in both English and Sinhala!
🔒 Strict Security Rules (Admin vs. Operator):
To keep your system safe from mistakes or unauthorized tampering, Front-Desk Security Guards (Duty Operators) are completely allowed to watch video streams and respond to alarms, but they are strictly blocked from accessing System Settings. If a guard clicks System Settings, a red "ACCESS DENIED" screen appears—protecting your passwords and master controls!

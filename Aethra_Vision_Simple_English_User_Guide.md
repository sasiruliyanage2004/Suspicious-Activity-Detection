# 🛡️ Aethra Vision: The Everyday User Guide
## Your Friendly, Easy-to-Understand Guide to Intelligent Video Security

---

### 👋 1. Welcome to Aethra Vision!
Thank you for using **Aethra Vision**! Think of this software as a **super-smart, tireless electronic security guard** that works alongside your regular CCTV video cameras 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

#### What makes this different from regular CCTV cameras?
* ❌ **Old Traditional Security Systems:** Regular CCTV cameras are simply "dumb video recorders." If a burglar jumps over your fence at 2:00 AM or someone leaves an abandoned package at a front door, old cameras just silently record it. You only find out the next morning when it is already too late!
* ✅ **Aethra Vision (Our Smart System):** Our software brings your ordinary cameras to life! Using artificial intelligence (AI), it watches the live video streams in real time. The exact moment a suspicious intruder enters a private zone, an aggressive cry is heard, or a weapon is spotted, the system **instantly raises an alarm, flashes red warning lights, speaks out loud in human speech (English & Sinhala), and notifies your phone!**

### 💻 2. What Equipment Do You Need? (Simple Checklist)
You do not need an expensive space-age supercomputer to run this application! If you are installing this at an office, school, factory, or bank, here is the simple checklist of what you need:

1. **Video Cameras:** Any standard network CCTV camera (often called IP cameras or ONVIF cameras) connected via an Internet network wire (Ethernet cable).
2. **A Regular Office Computer (PC or Laptop):** 
   * A computer with a normal processor (like an **Intel Core i5 or i7**).
   * **16 GB of Memory (RAM)** so the software runs smoothly without slowing down.
   * A basic graphics card (like an **NVIDIA RTX 3050 or 3060**) is highly recommended, as it acts as the "high-speed engine" that processes video 30 times every second!
3. **Hard Disk Storage (To save video clips):**
   * Storing continuous high-definition video takes around **27 Gigabytes per day** for each camera.
   * To keep video history safely stored for an entire month (30 days) across 4 to 8 cameras, we recommend buying a normal **4 TB or 8 TB Surveillance Hard Drive** (such as a WD Purple or Seagate SkyHawk drive). They cost about the same as a normal computer hard disk!

When you open the app in your web browser, you will see a simple navigation menu on the left side of your screen. We designed every button using everyday plain English so that anyone can learn it in 5 minutes!

#### 1. 📺 Live Video Dashboard
* This is your main daily "Command Center." You can see all your cameras playing live on the screen at the same time.
* Want to see the real layout of your building? Click the **🗺️ INTERACTIVE CAMPUS BLUEPRINT** button at the bottom to flip from video squares into a real-life architectural map of your facility!

#### 2. 🔌 Connect & Setup Cameras
* Need to attach a brand-new camera you just bought? Click the **⚡ Auto-Discover Network Cameras** button. The app will automatically probe your internet cables, find the new camera, and turn on AI protection in seconds!

#### 3. 📁 Recorded Video Clips (With Smart Search!)
* This is where all past 24/7 continuous videos are safely archived hour by hour.
* **🤖 Superpower Feature:** Instead of sitting for hours fast-forwarding through boring videos to find an incident, just use our **AI Smart Video Search** bar! Simply type normal English words like: *"Show me the person wearing a red shirt running near the gate between 2 PM and 4 PM"*. The AI will find the exact second it happened!

#### 4. 🚨 Security Alert Logs
* Every time the system detects a suspicious person or alarm, it saves a permanent, tamper-proof record here with the date, time, camera name, and a snapshot photo of what happened.

#### 5. 📊 AI Detection Statistics
* A visual overview of how well your facility is protected over the past week or month.
* Want to show a report to your company owner, university professors, or police judges? Just click the **📥 DOWNLOAD OFFICIAL PDF REPORT** button! It creates a clean, official printable document automatically!

#### 6. 🛡️ Restricted Zones Setup
* Want to allow people to walk in the lobby, but forbid anyone from coming near the heavy vault door after dark?
* Go here, select a camera, and use your mouse to click and drag a **Red or Yellow Protective Box** right over the door in the video! When you click **Save & Activate Zone**, the system will immediately ring an alarm if any human crosses that box!

#### 7. ⚙️ System Settings
* This is where Master Administrators set passwords, activate two-factor security codes (Telegram/Google OTP), and configure company rules.

### 🌟 4. Special Superpowers You Can Use Anytime!

#### 🌙 Night Vision & 🔥 Thermal Infrared Mode
* Are your cameras monitoring a pitch-black parking lot or dark alleyway?
* Look at any live video box on your dashboard and click the small button at the top that says **⚡ RGB NORMAL**. 
* With one click, your video turns into military-grade **🌙 NVG IR-GREEN (Night Vision Goggle Mode)** so you can clearly see human shapes in dark lighting! Click it again to see **🔥 THERMAL MAP (Heat Sensor View)**!

#### 🎙️ Acoustic Scream & Glass Break Audio Alarms
* Sometimes, an attack starts *before* a person steps in front of a camera lens!
* Aethra Vision actively listens to microphones. If it hears breaking window glass, aggressive screaming, or gunshots, it instantly alerts the dashboard with an **Audio Acoustic Warning!**

### 🔐 5. Who is Allowed to Do What? (Safety & Security)
To protect your building against insider mistakes or unauthorized access, the app divides users into two safe groups:

1. **👑 Master Administrator (Facility Owners & Managers):**
   * Can view everything, export PDF reports, create restricted zones, provision new guards, and open **System Settings**.
2. **🛡️ Duty Operator (Front-Desk Security Guards & Watchmen):**
   * Can monitor all live videos, respond to alerts, and watch recorded WORM clips.
   * **Strict Protection:** Security Guards are **completely blocked from accessing System Settings**. If an operator attempts to open settings, the screen immediately turns red and says **"🔒 ACCESS DENIED"**, preventing anyone from changing passwords or disabling security protections!

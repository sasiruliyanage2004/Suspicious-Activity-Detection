01

Why this beats an ordinary CCTV camera

A normal camera records silently and says nothing. If someone climbs your fence at 3 AM, a traditional system simply stores the footage — you only find out the next morning. Aethra Vision turns every camera you already own into an awake, reasoning guard.

Instant alarm notification

The moment a human shape enters a restricted area, the system recognises it and raises an on-screen alarm immediately.

Human voice warnings

Clear spoken warnings in English and Sinhala play automatically to alert guards and deter intruders in real time.

Audio threat listening

The system listens for aggressive shouting or breaking glass — flagging danger before anyone steps in front of a lens.

02

What equipment you actually need

No supercomputers required. Aethra Vision runs on reliable, standard office-grade hardware you can buy off the shelf.

Standard network cameras

Any modern IP CCTV camera on Ethernet (RTSP / ONVIF). No proprietary hardware required.

A regular office computer

Intel Core i5/i7, 16 GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GPU (RTX 3050 or 3060 class) runs the AI smoothly in real time.

Hard disk storage

About 27 GB per camera per day. A 4–8 TB surveillance drive archives over a month across 4–8 cameras.

Smart motion storage

With Smart Motion recording enabled, footage is only saved during activity — cutting drive usage by up to 65%.

04

One-click superpowers

Night vision & thermal map

On any live feed, click the RGB NORMAL switch to flip instantly between military-grade Night Vision Green and full Heatmap Thermal — so you can spot people in total darkness.

Emergency facility lockdown

Top-right glowing red button. One click triggers red strobe flashers across the app and broadcasts emergency sirens with spoken warnings, in both English and Sinhala.

05

Who's allowed to touch what

Admins vs. duty operators

Front-desk duty operators can freely watch live video and respond to alarms, but are strictly blocked from System Settings. If an operator tries to open that screen, a red ACCESS DENIED notice appears — keeping passwords and master controls safe from accidental changes.
